# Harbor audience and message

## Working brief
For Omarchy users who want private conversations with a small circle, Harbor
should make chatting and switching computers feel like an ordinary part of the
desktop. Lead with belonging on Omarchy and continuity across machines.

Product direction, confirmed by Tyler on 23 October 2026: simultaneous linked
machines, synchronized conversations, optional self-hosted encrypted mailbox,
existing history on new devices, on-demand attachments and encrypted recovery.
These are planned capabilities. Current evidence is [here](docs/HARBOR.md);
required behavior is [the specification](docs/SPECIFICATION.md).

## Voice and claims
Plain, calm, concrete English. Explain what the person can do and what happens
next. Use familiar terms: identity, linked device, conversation, backup, mailbox.
Avoid nautical metaphors in operational instructions and encryption jargon in
ordinary flows. Never promise absolute security, anonymous metadata, remote
erasure, unrecoverable-message recovery or current multi-device support.
Distinguish encrypted transit/mailbox/backup from local storage protection.

Future-facing example: “Your conversations, on your Omarchy machines. Link a
computer you trust and pick up where you left off.” Publish this as a product
claim only after the specified journeys are verified.

## Basis and boundaries
This is owner-directed audience and product meaning, not a market study or
commercial offer. No pricing, acquisition campaign, outreach or publication is
part of preparation. Audience needs above reflect Tyler's stated choices rather
than independently measured demand. [juice.md](juice.md) translates them into
experience; it does not expand the product scope.
